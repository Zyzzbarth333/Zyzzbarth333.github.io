Ball joint and mount for a security camera

Personal project · 2022

The ball joint and screw mount on my eufyCam 2C Pro security camera had worn out, so I designed a replacement in Fusion 360 and 3D printed it.

My role
Design and print
When
Jun – Jul 2022
Tools
Fusion 360, FDM printing, Polymaker PolyMAX PLA
Files
On Printables (opens in a new tab)
On this page:
  • What I built

  • Result

What I built

  • The parts are printed in Polymaker PolyMAX PLA, which I chose for its strength.
  • I converted the camera's imperial outer thread to metric dimensions so the printed part screws onto it.
  • I tightened the ball joint's groove so the camera holds its position and stops drifting.
  • I offset the inner thread by 1 mm so the parts lock tight when screwed together.

Specifications

SizeBall joint 21 × 21 × 29 mm; base 40 × 40 × 15 mm

Result

The printed mount went into use in July 2022, and the model is published on Printables for anyone with the same camera.
