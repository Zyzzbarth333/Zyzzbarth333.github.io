About me

I'm studying mechatronic engineering at UQ (BE(Hons)/ME, finishing in 2027), and I like building things that have to work outside the lab.

At UQ Space I wrote the autonomy stack and ground control station for Theseus, our rover, and the team placed 1st of 17 at the 2026 Australian Rover Challenge. On the rocket side I wrote HAVOC-2's flight firmware and built COSMOS, the ground station the team runs at launches.

I led the avionics team until November 2026, when I handed the lead over to make room for my research assistant role and a Master's placement in Semester 1, 2027. I've stayed on the team as an engineer and support the new Lead and Deputy Lead in an advisory capacity. I'm still working on the rocket and rover ground control stations, PCB design and the Theseus VI autonomy stack.

I also work part-time as a research assistant in UQ's School of EECS, on PX4 flight-control software and modelling. The part of engineering I enjoy most is modelling: taking a real system and building a model that predicts how it will behave.
